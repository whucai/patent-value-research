
\section{Technological Knowledge Evolution Framework}

We conceptualize technological knowledge evolution as a sequential but interacting process.

Knowledge Acquisition represents how universities absorb existing technological knowledge from previous inventions and external sources.

Knowledge Recombination captures the generation of novel relationships among technological elements.

Knowledge Emergence identifies the formation and growth of new technological directions.

Knowledge Diffusion measures how technological knowledge spreads across organizations and fields.

Knowledge Transformation represents the long-term transition of technological trajectories toward new knowledge structures.

Each university is represented by an evolutionary profile:

\[
K_i=(A_i,R_i,E_i,D_i,T_i)
\]

where A, R, E, D, and T denote acquisition, recombination, emergence, diffusion, and transformation.
