
\section{Related Work}

\subsection{Patent-based Innovation Measurement}

Prior research has examined patent value using citations, novelty, technological scope, and family size.

\subsection{Knowledge Recombinant Innovation}

Innovation often emerges from recombination of existing knowledge elements. 
Patent networks provide opportunities to trace such processes.

\subsection{University Innovation Trajectories}

Existing university evaluations emphasize outputs, while less attention is paid to heterogeneous technological trajectories.
