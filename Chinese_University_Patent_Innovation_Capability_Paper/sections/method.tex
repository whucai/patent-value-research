
\section{Method}

\subsection{Patent as Technological Knowledge Object}

Each patent is represented as a multidimensional knowledge object:

\[
p_i=(Content,Relation,Time)
\]

including technological classifications, semantic information, citation relationships, and temporal position.

\subsection{Knowledge Evolution Measurement}

Knowledge Acquisition is measured through knowledge source diversity.

Knowledge Recombination is measured through novel technological combinations and semantic distance.

Knowledge Emergence combines novelty, growth, and recognition:

\[
E=Novelty \times Growth \times Recognition
\]

Knowledge Diffusion considers breadth, depth, and velocity of knowledge spread.

Knowledge Transformation captures trajectory shifts between technological evolution states.

\subsection{Trajectory Identification}

Gaussian mixture models identify evolutionary trajectory types.

Markov transition models analyze movement between trajectories:

\[
P_{ij}=Pr(C_t=j|C_{t-1}=i)
\]

\subsection{Validation}

The framework is validated using temporal prediction, external impact indicators, and robustness analysis.
