
\section{Results}

\subsection{Technological Knowledge Evolution Landscape}

The analysis reveals that university technological knowledge is organized into interconnected knowledge spaces rather than isolated patent outputs.

\subsection{Evolutionary Trajectories}

Four major trajectories are identified:

\textbf{Knowledge Maintenance Path}: continuous reinforcement of existing technological advantages.

\textbf{Knowledge Expansion Path}: broadening technological coverage through accumulation.

\textbf{Knowledge Recombination Path}: creating novel technological combinations.

\textbf{Knowledge Transformation Path}: transitioning toward new technological paradigms.

\subsection{Trajectory Transitions}

Longitudinal analysis shows that recombination-oriented trajectories often serve as intermediate stages toward technological transformation.
